Sources and browser observations
Checked on 8 October 2026

Carl Dev’s official itch.io page identifies the game as a released HTML5 game and describes four CCTV feeds and purging corrupted frequencies.

The browser build linked by that page was build 18906721. Its supplied source identifies the camera labels and the selected-camera check in the purge action.

What the browser agent actually did

The agent opened the developer’s build, selected CAM 01–04, waited for an anomaly to appear naturally, switched away, returned to the affected camera and clicked PURGE SIGNAL. The warning persisted on the other feed; a successful purge removed the entity and changed the status to SIGNAL: STABLE. No game state was injected for this observation.

The observation covers this sequence and this build. It does not verify an optimal strategy, long-run survival, mobile play or future updates. Game screenshots on this site were captured during that check; the game and its visuals belong to Carl Dev.
